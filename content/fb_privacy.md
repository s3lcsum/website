## Who we are

This site (`dominiksiejak.pl`) and the services offered under it (including the
Messenger assistant) are operated by Dominik Siejak as a personal, non-commercial
home-lab project.

## What data we process

- **Messenger assistant**: when you send a message to the assistant via Facebook
  Messenger, the text of your message is processed by an automated assistant to
  answer your question or operate home-automation devices. The assistant also
  retains a short-term conversation history for context.
- **Website**: this website does not use tracking cookies or analytics beyond what
  the hosting provider (Cloudflare) collects for security and availability (standard
  web logs).

## How we use data

Your Messenger messages are used **only** to (a) answer you, (b) operate the
home-automation devices you explicitly ask to control, and (c) improve the
assistant. We do **not** sell, rent, or share message content with third parties.

## How long we keep data

Message text and conversation history are kept only as long as needed to provide
the service and then deleted. Standard hosting logs are retained for security and
troubleshooting in line with Cloudflare's policies.

## Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, contact the operator using the contact address on
this site. For Messenger, you can also delete the conversation on your side; message
history held by the assistant is deleted on request within a reasonable time.

## Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, use the contact details shown on the
site's homepage.
